# Macro Action Bar v1.0.0

The first major release. Macros now live on your action bars as well as in the spellbook: right-click one on a bar to change it there. Every casting option has been reworked into clearer menus, and every class page has been reorganised.

## Upgrading

- **The addon is now called Macro Action Bar** (it was Macro Spellbook). The folder and `.toc` are now `MacroActionBar`, and the saved variables are `MacroActionBarDB` and `MacroActionBarCharDB`. Remove the old `MacroSpellbook` folder when you install this version.
- Because the saved variables were renamed, your macro settings start fresh. Copy macros from older versions ("Fireball @F", "Mark of the Wild @N", "Arcane Explosion R1") are no longer used. Focus, Nearest Player and Rank 1 Out Of Combat are now options on the spell's own macro, so you can delete the old copies in `/macro`.

## Action bars

- Right-click one of the addon's macros on Blizzard's action bars (out of combat) to open its macro menu. Spells placed straight from Blizzard's spellbook open their spell's macro menu too, and the first change swaps the macro in.
- **Macro variants:** a macro on several buttons stays shared until you change it on one button. That button then gets its own variant ("Polymorph #2"). A variant that ends up identical to another macro of the same spell merges back into it.
- **Duplicate Macro** (on the page) and **Delete Macro** (on variants) in the menu.
- Deleting a macro empties the action bar slots it was on, including macros deleted outside the addon.

## Casting options

- **Cast On:** one choice per macro: Target, Mouseover, Focus, Cursor or Nearest Player. Focus and Mouseover fall back to your target. Nearest Player (for buffs) targets the nearest friendly player, or you when no one is near, so a buff never lands on an NPC.
- Every ranged harmful spell offers Mouseover automatically, without being listed in the class config.
- **Rank:** Normal or Downrank In Raids, plus a **Rank 1 Out Of Combat** checkbox (rank 1 out of combat, highest rank in combat). Both are greyed out while a Sequence is on.
- **Cast On Enemy Player** (Dispel Magic, Purge): a second macro that targets the nearest enemy player, casts, and goes back to your target.
- **Before Casting / After Casting** menus add lines on either side of the cast: Stop Casting (before only), Start Attack, Stop Attack, Pet Attack, Pet Follow, your racials, your equipped on-use items, off-GCD spells (e.g. Presence of Mind before Pyroblast), Warrior stances, Hunter Auto Shot, Mage Cancel Ice Block, and Clear Dead Target and Target Nearest Enemy. Lines are written in the order you tick them, and the menu shows each one's position. When no on-use item is equipped, the menu shows a greyed-out "No On-Use Item Equipped" line.
- **Sequence:** builds a `/castsequence` for the spells it allows. Cooldown spells offer **Then Cast** instead: pick a spell to cast right after the cooldown on the same press.
- **Shift, Ctrl and Alt:** pick another spell, or an equipped on-use item, for the macro to use while that key is held. Each picked spell has its own Cast On (Target, plus Mouseover, Focus or Cursor where the spell offers them).
- **Forms and stances:** Druids can pick a different spell for each form (Shred in Cat, Maul in Bear), and Warriors for each stance. A spell is only offered in a form or stance it can be cast in.
- **Recast options,** so a button can be spammed: Stay In Stealth On Recast, Don't Recast While Channeling, Stay In Form On Recast (Druid forms), and Don't Toggle Off On Recast (Auto Shot, aspects, Shadowform).
- **Reset To Default** clears every setting on a macro. It is greyed out when nothing differs from the default.

## Equipment

- Equipped items with a "Use:" effect (trinkets, engineering tinkers, etc.) get their own Equipment section. Each macro uses the slot, not the item, so it keeps working after a gear swap.
- Equipment macros can Stop Attacking and Stop Casting before use.

## Page and settings

- The Macro Action Bar tab now sits down the spellbook's right edge, so other addons' launchers and native tabs no longer push it around.
- The page fits the updated Forever spellbook, including its one- and two-page modes.
- A search box filters the page by macro or section name.
- Hold Alt over a page macro to see the spell's (or item's) own tooltip instead of the macro text. Custom macros can have a description, also shown while Alt is held.
- Macros longer than 255 characters are flagged in their tooltip and are never written.
- **Global Macro Settings** in the settings dropdown: Clear Dead Target and Target Nearest Enemy for every enemy spell, and Auto Shot after spell cast for Hunters. A macro's own setting still wins.
- **Open spellbook to this page:** opening the spellbook shows the Macro Action Bar page instead of the spell page.
- **Minimap button:** left-click opens the page, right-click can hide it, and drag moves it. Turn it back on in Options > AddOns > Macro Action Bar.
- Menu options are title-cased throughout.

## Macro slots

- Macros the addon makes in the shared General tab (once a character's own tab is full) no longer pile up for every character. On a fresh login that finds the General tab full, the addon clears its General macros and puts back the ones on this character's action bars, in their slots.
- The "macros full" message also shows a popup, with a "Don't show this again" box.

## Classes

- **Druid:** Cat, Bear and Forms sections, with Custom listed first. Travel and Aquatic forms, Shifting Power, and a **Smart Travel** macro (Cat Form and Dash indoors, Aquatic Form when swimming, Travel Form otherwise).
- **Hunter:** Traps, Aspects and Tracking sections. **One Button Tracking** (tracks your target's creature type) and **Pet Attack/Recall** macros.
- **Mage:** Arcane, Fire and Frost sections, plus Conjure and Teleports & Portals. Arcane Wrath and a **Spam Wand** macro.
- **Paladin:** Blessings and Greater Blessings in their own section.
- **Priest:** Power Infusion, Inner Focus, Shadowform and Vampiric Embrace (shown with Show Unlearned until learned). Shadowform can be spammed without dropping out. **Spam Wand** macro.
- **Rogue:** Assassination, Combat and Subtlety sections, and a Poisons section.
- **Shaman:** totems grouped into Earth, Fire, Water and Air sections. Water Breathing and Water Walking moved to Buffs & Defence.
- **Warlock:** Affliction and Destruction sections, Summon Imp, and a **Pet Attack/Recall** macro.
- **Warrior:** Arms, Fury and Protection sections. Stances replace the old Switch To option (see Forms and stances above).
- Healer pages (Druid, Paladin, Priest, Shaman) list Damage before Healing, like every other class.

## Fixes

- The search box shows its "Search" placeholder again.
- Custom macros whose config icon is a full texture path can be picked up again.
